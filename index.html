Color removal sounds simple, but edges, compression, and shadows can create dozens of nearby pixel values. These controls help you remove the right range.

What does “remove color” mean?

The selected color becomes transparent. The tool compares every pixel with your sample and removes pixels that fall inside the tolerance range. The visible result is a PNG with transparent areas where that color used to be.

This works especially well for flat logos, icons, scanned signatures, product graphics, and illustrations with a solid background.

How tolerance works

A tolerance of zero targets only pixels that exactly match the chosen RGB value. Raise the value to include similar shades caused by anti-aliasing, lighting, shadows, or JPEG compression.

Start low and increase gradually. If important parts of the subject disappear, reduce the tolerance or sample a more representative background pixel.

Make a background color transparent

If an image has a single-color background, click a clean background area and watch the preview. The checkerboard shows which pixels are transparent. Edge softness creates a gentler transition around the subject.

This tool removes colors by similarity. It is not an AI subject detector, so complex multicolor backgrounds may need a dedicated background-removal or photo-editing tool.

Why the download is PNG

JPEG files cannot store transparent pixels. Even when you open a JPG, the cleaned result downloads as PNG so the transparent areas remain transparent in design tools, websites, presentations, and documents.

Keep your source image. Exporting creates a new file and does not overwrite the original on your device.

Common questions

Image color remover FAQ

How do I remove one color from an image?

Upload an image, click the color you want to remove, adjust the tolerance until the preview looks right, then download the result as a transparent PNG.

Can I make a color transparent without Photoshop?

Yes. This browser-based tool removes a selected color without installing Photoshop or creating an account.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing happens locally in your browser, so your files do not need to be uploaded to our server.

What image formats are supported?

You can open common browser-supported image formats such as PNG, JPG, JPEG and WebP. Results download as PNG so transparent pixels are preserved.

What does color tolerance do?

Tolerance controls how similar a pixel must be to the selected color before it becomes transparent. A low value removes a narrow color range; a higher value also removes nearby shades.

Can I remove a specific color from a PNG?

Yes. Open the PNG, click the specific color, adjust tolerance for nearby shades, and download a new PNG with that color made transparent.

Is this the same as an AI background remover?

No. This tool removes pixels by color similarity. It is precise for solid colors, logos, icons and simple backgrounds. An AI background remover identifies a subject and is usually better for complex photographic scenes.
